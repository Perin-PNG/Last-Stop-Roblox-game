## 2. Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire

Creator Hub > your experience > Audience > Maturity & Compliance. Answer it yourself for
the most intense thing in the game. What the game actually contains, so you can answer
accurately:

- **Fear:** frequent jumpscares (the Clerk, mimic reveals, the Latecomer boarding, the
  KnockFace), sustained tension through every stop, psychological horror (the Driver in
  the mirror, Loop checks, The Between, mimics that look like people). Eerie audio
  throughout once sounds are in. The plan expects this to come out **Moderate**.
- **Violence:** being caught is static and a fade. No blood, gore or weapons. The camera
  is first person.
- **No:** blood, gore, crude humour, romance, alcohol or drugs shown, gambling, paid
  random items, real brands.
- **Chat and voice:** the game uses only Roblox's own chat. Voice, if you turn it on, is
  read only as a loudness level on the player's own device, never recorded.
- **Purchases:** list whatever passes and products you actually create.

Then check the label it gives. Any later update that changes an answer means submitting
it again.

## 3. Store page

- [ ] **Name:** Don't Look at the Driver
- [ ] **Description** (edit freely):

      Don't miss the bus. Don't look at the driver.

      The 9N night bus runs one route through Hollow County, and it never waits. Ride
      with up to four friends, step off at each stop to find fuel and batteries before
      the departure bell, and get back aboard. Follow the Rules on the plaque. Some of
      them are fake.

      Miss the bus and you walk The Between. Let the wrong passenger aboard and it
      isn't a passenger. Reach the Terminal with five fragments and you might see dawn.

      - Co-op for 1 to 4 players
      - A new route every night: 11 stops from 18
      - A solo First Ride that teaches the Rules
      - Three endings, and one more you have to find

      4. A mimic revealed in the aisle.
      5. The finale drive out of the Terminal at dawn.
- [ ] **A trailer clip** (30 seconds or less) from the same moments.
