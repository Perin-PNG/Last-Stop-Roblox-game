# M3 playtest: the alpha

**Goal:** a new player can learn the game in the First Ride, and a full run of all 11
stops reaches the Terminal and plays the finale to one of the three good endings.

Before you start: API access on (see STUDIO-TODO). To see the First Ride offer again, use
a fresh test account, or press **Reset First Ride and hints** in the debug panel (**DBG** or F8).

## 1. First launch

1. Press Play. The **brightness calibration** screen shows once: move the slider until
   the left symbol is barely visible, then **Done**.
2. The **First Ride** offer pops up in the depot. Press **Later**. Check you can still
   start it from the green **FIRST RIDE** sign on the platform.

## 2. The First Ride (about six minutes, can't be failed)

A green objective bar at the top walks you through it:

1. Read the glowing Rules plaque by the front door (look at it for a couple of seconds).
2. Look at the Driver in the mirror. The face starts to turn. Look away.
3. **Gas & Go:** get off, grab a battery and a fuel can, pour the fuel at the cap on the
   bus's rear left corner, get back aboard before the bell.
4. **The Laundromat:** when the streetlights go out, get aboard and pull the cord twice.
5. **Gas & Go again:** the clerk is facing the wall. Stay aboard and pull the cord twice.

Check: each hint shows once. A second First Ride doesn't repeat them.

## 3. The full route

Board the 9N for a normal run. All 11 stops, with three route votes per tier. New in M3:

- **Tier 3 stops:** Level P∞ (the parking garage that only goes down), Low Water Station
  (keep moving in the water), the Bus Graveyard (check the route number and the Driver's
  cap before boarding the Other Bus), the Station With No Name (don't follow the
  Attendant), and Elm & 3rd again (the Resident can't ping).
- **The Terminal**, if you have five fragments. Without five, the run goes into overtime.

## 4. The finale

1. The Driver walks off the bus. The depot is dark.
2. Restore the **three breakers** while roamers hunt you. The exit gate opens.
3. Someone takes the **driver's seat**. Everyone else has eight seconds to get aboard.
4. **Drive out:** W/S or the triggers to speed up and slow down, A/D or the stick to
   steer. Dodge the wrecks, pour fuel into the tank feed when it leaks, pry hands off the
   doors, and stay ahead of the Latecomer.
5. Dawn. Check the ending: **Home** (everyone made it), **Transfer** (someone was lost),
   or the secret one.

## 5. Settings and menus

- **Settings:** effects and music volume, Reduce scares, Sound indicators (arrows for
  sounds), Head bob, FOV, sensitivity, and Calibrate again.
- **Journal:** your rank, endings seen, Driver clues and lore you found.
- **Catalog:** every anomaly you photographed, grouped by stop.
- **Route:** the stops you've visited.
- On a phone, the **Menu** touch button opens it. On a gamepad, Select opens it, the
  d-pad moves, B closes it.
